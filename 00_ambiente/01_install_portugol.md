<h1>Portugol Studio Installation Guide</h1>

Portugol Studio is software that will help the developer in the learning process and adaptation to programming commands and structures.

1. Access the website: [http://lite.acad.univali.br/portugol/](https://univali-lite.github.io/Portugol-Studio/) and click the <b>Download</b> button

<div align="center"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/0lL5LJd.png"></div>

3. When running this installer, we need to allow its execution in the operating system. Check if this permission request has appeared on your taskbar or desktop. At this stage, we need to confirm this permission by clicking <b>"YES"</b>

4. The installer will display its welcome screen. Here we must continue by clicking <b>Next</b>

<div align="center"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/eBMYKZ6.png" alt="portugol-instala-o-1" border="0" width="80%"></div>

4. On the next screen, check the option: <b>"I accept the terms of the agreement"</b> and click <b>Next</b>

<div align="center"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/WmSUsYS.png" alt="portugol-instala-o-2" border="0" width="80%"></div>

5. We will proceed to the software presentation screen. Here we can click <b>Next</b>

<div align="center"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/n2b5YFE.png" alt="portugol-instala-o-3" border="0" width="80%"></div>

6. Following the LGPD guidelines, the software will present the terms of use. Click <b>Next</b>

<div align="center"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/VbFzR60.png" alt="portugol-instala-o-4" border="0" width="80%"></div>

9. Wait for the installation process, where we will monitor the progress bar 

<div align="center"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/I5RQS5L.png" alt="portugol-instala-o-5" border="0" width="80%"></div>

10. The next screen is only informative, about opening files. You can choose between Console or Graphic execution modes. In our sessions, we will use the Graphic mode. Click <b>Next</b>

<div align="center"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/Zv85pgv.png" alt="portugol-instala-o-6" width="80%" border="0"></div>

11. We will then proceed to the screen below, which is the finalization screen that should display the message "Portugol Studio setup has been completed on your computer". Click <b>Finish</b>

<div align="center"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/LNaUdY8.png" alt="portugol-instala-o-7" border="0" width="80%"></div>

Following the steps described above, we have completed the installation of Portugol Studio, which can be located on your machine through the search box on your taskbar, or in the start menu of your operating system.

<img src="https://i.ibb.co/GHd5PWY/portugol-instala-o-8-JPG.png" alt="portugol-instala-o-8-JPG" border="0">
